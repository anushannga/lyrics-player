# Asset notes

Project source and packaging were prepared for Pasan Anushanga (`anushannga`).
Song titles and performers identify the local recordings used for timing;
this repository does not include MP3 recordings.

The project uses user-supplied Sinhala font files: ISI Gurulugomi,
ISI Disapamok, and THARU DIGITAL NIKINI. Font files and lyric text retain their
respective owners' rights. No new font or music license is asserted here.

Arduino and U8g2 are external dependencies and are not vendored in this repository.
Their own license terms apply. No repository-wide open-source license has been
added on behalf of owners of the bundled third-party assets.
